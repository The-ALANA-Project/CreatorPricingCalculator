PUBLIC FOLDER - Add Your Images Here
=====================================

This folder needs two image files:

1. favicon.png - Your "S" logo (the circular pink icon)
2. og-image.png - Your social sharing banner ("CREATOR PRICING CALCULATOR")

HOW TO GET THEM:
-----------------
Open /generate-public-assets.html in your browser to download both images,
then add them to this folder.

These files will be copied directly to your deployed site and used for:
- Browser favicon (tab icon)
- Social media preview images (when sharing links)

The HTML file already references these correctly!
